What did this synthetic dataset miss?

  • Direction and strength. Sleep duration rises with age in the synthetic file. The survey shows a weak association in the opposite direction.
  • Variability. Synthetic sleep durations range from 5.8 to 8.5 hours. Survey responses span a wider range, though the survey records sleep in whole hours.
  • Group sizes. The synthetic file has only ten records in the obesity category and none in the underweight category.

Why does accurate synthetic data matter?

  • Prototyping. Synthetic records can help teams build and test software when real records are hard to share.
  • Model behavior. A model trained on synthetic data can learn relationships that hardly appear in real data.
  • Validation. Compare group sizes, variability and relationships with real data suited to the task.

Data and methods

Sources and cohort
We kept all records in the challenge CSV. We joined six public data files by participant ID within each of the 2009–2010 and 2011–2012 NHANES cycles. We kept adults aged 27–59 who had a job or business the previous week and a valid sleep response.
Sleep and BMI
Short sleep means under seven hours. NHANES records usual weekday or workday sleep in whole hours, with 12 meaning 12 or more. We merged the synthetic labels “Normal” and “Normal Weight.” For measured NHANES BMI, we used CDC’s adult categories with cutoffs at 18.5, 25 and 30.
Calculations and missing data
Percentages use the records with values for the measures shown. Pearson correlations use complete pairs. For pulse and first systolic readings, we excluded missing and nonpositive values. NHANES counts vary by measure. BMI uses , pulse , systolic pressure and activity .

Checks and limitations

Activity measures
For each intensity, we multiplied NHANES leisure activity days per week by minutes per day, then added moderate and vigorous minutes. A “no” contributes zero and incomplete responses are excluded. The synthetic file uses daily activity minutes, so we compare the direction of the gaps within each dataset.

Scope and reproduction
These are unadjusted associations in an unweighted survey sample. The analysis cannot establish cause and effect or give national estimates. From the project folder, run python3 analyze.py /path/to/nhanes-cache with pandas to rebuild the figures. See NIST’s synthetic data guidance for context.
